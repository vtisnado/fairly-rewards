# Demo video script (90 seconds)

Record on https://fairly.disenoapps.dev with two screens side by side: a phone (customer) and a tablet or second browser window (counter). Speak slowly; captions recommended.

| Time | Screen | Action | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Landing | Scroll the hero slowly | "Loyalty cards are usually points you forget about. Fairly gives you cashback cards with real money inside, on NEAR." |
| 0:08–0:12 | Landing | Tap **Try the 5-minute demo** | "Let's try it at a demo café." |
| 0:12–0:22 | Phone | Step 1: **Get a card at Cafe Esquina** → wallet opens with the card | "One tap and I have a card. No wallet app, no seed phrase, no gas: the key lives in the browser and the app sponsors the transactions." |
| 0:22–0:30 | Phone | Tap the center **Show my code** button | "At the counter I show one code for all my cards. It rotates every three minutes, so a screenshot stops working." |
| 0:30–0:42 | Counter | Step 2: demo counter → scan the code → enter $24 → confirm | "The cashier scans it, enters the purchase, and 5% cashback lands on my card on chain." |
| 0:42–0:50 | Phone | Card shows the new balance and stamp; tap the card to open details | "The balance is real test money, backed by the café's rewards pool." |
| 0:50–1:02 | Phone | **Sell this card** → set a price → it appears on the market | "And unlike points, I can sell the card. It's a NEAR NFT, and the café earns a royalty on every resale." |
| 1:02–1:15 | Desktop | Step 3: sample business dashboard → sales chart, customers, boosts | "The business sees sales, returning customers and cashback runway, and pays for rewards after it sells, in a weekly settlement." |
| 1:15–1:24 | Desktop | Back to landing → **Start your business** | "Any business can start its own program in minutes on testnet: set the cashback, connect a counter, print the QR." |
| 1:24–1:30 | Landing | End on the logo and URL | "Fairly. Loyalty that pays real money back. fairly.disenoapps.dev" |

## Before recording
- Open the customer wallet once beforehand so the intro slides are already seen, or skip them on camera.
- Use a fresh browser profile for the phone so the card is new.
- Check that the café pool has funds (the dashboard runway is not red), so the cashback is Available and the card can be sold.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb on both devices.

## Shots to avoid
- Private keys, backup files and the `.env`.
- Browser address bars showing anything other than the production URL.
