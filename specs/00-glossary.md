# Glossary (approved)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Merchant | Account that owns a program and funds its pool |
| Program | A merchant's loyalty program, identified by a unique `slug` |
| Pool | tUSDC held by `fairly` on behalf of a program, used to pay cashback |
| Cashier | Account authorized by the merchant to issue passes and record purchases |
| Pass | NEP-171 NFT belonging to one program; holds a balance and a tier |
| Balance | tUSDC credited to a pass as cashback, spendable via redeem |
| Tier | Level of a pass computed from its purchase count (e.g. Bronze, Silver, Gold) |
| Perk | A benefit unlocked at a tier (e.g. free coffee), honored by the merchant |
| Redemption | Spending pass balance or claiming a perk |
| Listing | A pass offered for resale at a price in tUSDC |
| Royalty | Percentage of a resale price paid to the merchant |
| Expiry | Return of a pass balance to the pool after inactivity |
| Sponsor | Server-side account that funds new customer accounts with testnet NEAR |
